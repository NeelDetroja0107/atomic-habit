Core Concepts

Four Laws That
Will Change
Everything

01 👁️

Make It Obvious

Design your environment to surface the cues for good habits. What you see shapes what you do. Restructure your surroundings so the right choice becomes the effortless one.

02 ✨

Make It Attractive

Bundle habits you need to do with things you want to do. The more appealing a habit is, the more likely it becomes part of your daily routine and identity.

03 ⚡

Make It Easy

Reduce friction to the smallest possible action. The Two-Minute Rule: if it takes less than two minutes, start there. Motion creates momentum. Simplicity is power.

04 🏆

Make It Satisfying

What is immediately rewarded is repeated. Give yourself a small reward at the end of a habit. Track your streak and never miss twice — consistency is the ultimate skill.
